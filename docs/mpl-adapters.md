# MPL-2.0 and separate adapters

CareerOps source files are covered by MPL-2.0. Modifications to covered files must satisfy the license's source-availability requirements when distributed.

Consumers may keep separate policy, provider, storage, deployment, and orchestration adapters under another license when those adapters remain separate files and merely use the public interfaces. Prefer composition over copying or modifying public source files.

This document is practical project guidance, not legal advice. Review the license and obtain qualified advice for a specific distribution model when needed.
