# Code of conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and avoid publishing another person's personal or confidential information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption, and unauthorized disclosure are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Report conduct concerns through a private channel when possible. Do not include sensitive personal details in public reports.
